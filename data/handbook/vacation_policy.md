# Vacation and Time-Off Policy (fictional)

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 30 vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a proportional amount. Vacation days are credited on January 1st.

## Requesting vacation
Vacation is requested in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. The team lead approves or rejects the request within three business days. Requests longer than ten consecutive days need approval from the department head.

## Carry-over
Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31st.

## Sick leave
Employees who are sick notify their team lead before 9:00 on the first day. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive sick day onwards.
