# Information Security Policy (fictional)

## Accounts
All employees must use the company password manager and two-factor authentication. Passwords are never shared, not even with colleagues from the same team.

## Security awareness training
Every employee completes the security awareness training. The training certificate is valid for 12 months and must then be renewed. Without a valid certificate, privileged access rights are suspended.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security officer within 1 hour. The security officer then decides whether the device is wiped remotely.

## Data classification
Company data is classified as public, internal, confidential or restricted. Customer data is always at least confidential and may only be stored on approved systems.
