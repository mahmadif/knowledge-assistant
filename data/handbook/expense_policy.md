# Expense and Travel Policy (fictional)

## Submitting expenses
Expenses are submitted in the finance portal together with the receipts. Receipts must be submitted within 14 days of the expense. Late submissions are only reimbursed with the approval of the finance team.

## Approval thresholds
Expenses of up to 500 EUR are approved by the team lead. Expenses above 500 EUR need the approval of the department head. Purchases above 5,000 EUR must go through the procurement department.

## Travel
Train travel is preferred for distances below 600 km. Flights in economy class are booked through the travel portal. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night.
