# Remote Work Policy (fictional)

## Remote days
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, during which employees are reachable for their team.

## Equipment
Employees receive a one-time equipment allowance of 300 EUR for their home office, for example for a monitor or a chair. Purchases above this amount follow the expense policy.

## Working abroad
Working from abroad is allowed for up to 20 days per year in EU countries. Other countries need the approval of the HR department.
