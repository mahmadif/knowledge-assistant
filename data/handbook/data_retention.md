# Data Retention Policy (fictional)

## Application logs
Application logs are retained for 90 days and are then deleted automatically. Security audit logs are retained for 12 months.

## Customer data
Customer data is deleted 30 days after the end of the contract. Customers can request earlier deletion in writing, which the support team forwards to the data protection officer.

## Backups
Backups are kept for 35 days. A deletion request is considered complete once the data has also expired from all backups.

## Exceptions
Legal holds override all retention periods. Only the legal department can create or lift a legal hold.
