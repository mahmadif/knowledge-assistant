# Access Management Policy (fictional)

## Standard access
Access to the code repository, the wiki and the ticket system is requested in the access portal. The team lead approves the request, and IT grants access within one business day.

## Production access
Production access requires the approval of the security officer and a valid security awareness training certificate. It is never granted during an employee's first 30 days. Production access is always granted for a specific team and purpose.

## Reviews
All access rights are reviewed every 90 days by the respective team lead. Access that is no longer needed is removed during the review.

## Offboarding
When an employee leaves the company, IT removes all access within 24 hours after the last working day.
