# On-Call Handbook (fictional)

## Rotation
The on-call rotation changes weekly. The handover takes place every Monday at 10:00 in the engineering channel. A secondary engineer is always assigned as backup.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive an allowance of 150 EUR per week. In addition, every callout outside working hours is compensated with time off in lieu.

## Escalation
If the primary on-call engineer does not respond, the alert escalates to the secondary engineer after 15 minutes and then to the engineering manager. Serious incidents are handled according to the incident response runbook.
